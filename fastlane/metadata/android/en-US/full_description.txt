Five, four, three, two, one, GO. Corner Cutters Rally is an arcade rally in a crisp pixel-3D look. You drive long special stages against the clock, one car at a time, while your co-driver reads out every bend ahead.

THE CO-DRIVER CALLS EVERY BEND
LEFT 4. RIGHT 2 TIGHTENS. HAIRPIN. OVER JUMP. TUNNEL. Every bend is graded from 6 (barely a lift) to 1 (the slowest), read out loud and shown on a card at the top of the screen. There's no map: trust the notes and keep it flat.

A SEASON, ONE ROUND A WEEK
Five rallies make a season, and a new round opens every week. The same nine rival crews race you all season. The top ten score points at every round, and the quickest five on the last stage, the Power Stage, take bonus points. Most points at the end takes the title.

FIVE RALLIES, SEVENTEEN STAGES
- Rally of the Forests: gravel roads through the pines and over the moor
- Winter Rally: snow and ice high in the mountains
- Desert Rally: sand, dust, palms and camels
- Tarmac Rally: mountain passes, vineyards, the coast and a castle hill
- Riviera Rally: tight cliff roads above the sea, with hairpins and tunnels
Every stage is a long road of its own, with jumps, crowds at the slow bends and a flying finish.

LOOK AFTER THE CAR
Damage carries over from stage to stage until the service park fixes it. Rivals have their bad days too: a spin, a puncture, a roll. Split times tell you where you stand.

TWO THUMBS, NOTHING ELSE
Hold the phone sideways and the whole screen is your wheel: hold the left side to turn left, the right side to turn right, and both to brake. The gas is always on, so all you think about is the line. Keyboards and gamepads drive the same way. Put your own name on the standings.

PICK UP AND PLAY
A short controls lap teaches the basics. Three difficulties, from a gentle EASY to a flat-out HARD.

FOR EVERYONE
Large text, colour-safe split times, screen shake you can turn off, and eight languages: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano, Türkçe and 日本語.

Free. No ads. No account. Plays offline.
