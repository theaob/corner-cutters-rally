Five, four, three, two, one, GO. Corner Cutters Rally is an arcade rally in a crisp pixel-3D look, made for a phone held upright. One thumb drives: point the way you want to go and the car goes there. Point hard and it drifts.

THE CO-DRIVER CALLS EVERY BEND
Your co-driver reads the road ahead and calls it out loud: LEFT 4, RIGHT 2 TIGHTENS, HAIRPIN, OVER JUMP. Every bend is graded from 6 (barely a lift) to 1 (the slowest), and a card at the top of the screen shows the call. Listen, trust the notes, and keep it flat.

FOUR RALLIES, FOURTEEN STAGES
- Rally of the Forests: gravel roads over the hills and through the pines
- Winter Rally: snow and ice up in the mountains
- Desert Rally: sand and dust under the desert sun
- Tarmac Rally: sealed mountain roads, vineyards, the coast and a castle hill
Every stage is a long point-to-point road of its own, with jumps, crowds at the hairpins and a flying finish.

BEAT NINE RIVAL CREWS
You run alone against the clock, but nine rival crews run every stage too. Split times show where you stand, and the standings after each stage show who leads the rally. Rivals make mistakes as well: a spin, a puncture, a roll. The least time overall wins.

LOOK AFTER THE CAR
Damage carries over from one stage to the next until the service park puts it right. Hit the trees too often and your rally gets a lot harder.

PICK UP AND PLAY
A short controls lap on the Shakedown stage teaches the basics. Three difficulties, from a gentle EASY to a flat-out HARD. Prefer the full sim? Switch STYLE to SIM for pedals, a chase camera and more views.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
Large text, colour-safe split times, a thumb that works anywhere on the lower screen (left- or right-handed), screen shake you can turn off, and gamepad and keyboard support.

Free. No ads. No account. Plays offline.
